Parkview Estate Extension II, Lagos

Live besidethe Atlantic.

322 waterfront residences, a 2,000 sqm clubhouse and a marina and boat club on 4.85 hectares of parkland.

Explore the residences → The waterfront

An urban retreat by the water.

The Atlantic View is conceived as a premium residential community where architecture, landscape and waterfront living come together as one experience.

Elegant mid-rise apartment buildings sit within generous landscaped open space, with active recreation, a central clubhouse and a marina-oriented lifestyle. Rather than treating the landscape as leftover space, the master plan makes greenery, gardens, pedestrian movement and leisure destinations part of the identity of the estate.

The result is a residential environment for people who value privacy, views, wellness, social connection and the atmosphere of life beside the water.

    The same berths, the same skyline, two moments of the day.

    By afternoon the marina is in use: boats leaving the docks, palms along the quay and the balconies open to the water. By dusk the blocks light up floor by floor and the whole estate is reflected in the marina.

    The residences.

    Each block holds 23 homes. On each of the seven typical floors, a 1 bedroom and a 2 bedroom apartment on the right of the floor and a 3 bedroom + 1 BQ apartment on the left; on Level 9, two more 3 bedroom + BQ apartments, one on either side. Across fourteen blocks, 322 residences.

      Eight floors: a double-height ground floor with the lobby, gym and parking, seven typical floors and a top floor of two 3 bedroom + BQ apartments, 40.5 metres to the roof.

      Inside, the water is never out of view.

      Pale stone, walnut joinery and full-height glass. Every main room is drawn towards the balcony and the Atlantic beyond.

      The lobby

      Arrival is through a double-height lobby under a sculpted ceiling of lit coves: a travertine and walnut reception desk against a slatted timber wall, a lounge for guests, terrazzo floors with brass inlays and the lifts set in walnut and brushed steel.

      Living rooms

      The living room runs the depth of the plan, from the entrance lobby to a wall of glass onto the balcony. A coffered ceiling with concealed light, a stone floor and a soft, pale palette keep the view as the focus.

      Kitchens

      Open to the living room, with a stone island for four, walnut joinery to the ceiling and glazing on two sides that looks out over the palms to the water.

      Bedrooms

      The suites turn to the water. Full-height glazing, sheer curtains, a seating corner and the terrace a step away.

      Bathrooms

      Lined in pale stone, with walk-in rain showers, walnut vanities and daylight from a window to the water.

      The gym

      Every block has its own gym on the ground floor: a double-height room under a timber ceiling, glazed from floor to ceiling onto the gardens, with cardio and strength zones and a separate equipment room. 85 m² of fitness floor and 100 m² of machines, as drawn.
