# The Atlantic View

Brochure website for The Atlantic View, a proposed luxury waterfront residential development at Parkview Estate Extension II, Ikoyi, Lagos (architects A365 Designs). One static page: `index.html` with `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/site.js` and the generated `assets/js/plan-data.js`.

- `assets/r/` lightbox renders (2200 px tall / 2560 wide), `assets/m/` page renders at 2x (1600 tall / 2000 wide) with `assets/s/` 1x copies (900 / 1100) served through `srcset`, `assets/t/` thumbnails (800 / 900), `assets/hero.jpg` and `hero-s.jpg` landscape hero crops (cut from the September marina-dusk render; the hero is left alone by later render rebuilds). Since 2026-09-30 the day views come from `../New-Renders/` and the gallery carries 34 Option 1 views including nine interiors. Since 2026-10-01 the fourteen landscape renders of the second design option in `../Design-Option-2/` are built as `o2-*` slugs (1920/1400/640 px wide) for the "Two design options" section, the gallery's Option 2 switch, and Option 2 panels, steps, previews and thumbnails in the waterfront strip, the architecture scrolly, the experience index and the arrival list. Three lobby renders (`New-Renders/Interior1-3.png`, slugs lobby-1/2/3) lead the interiors stack.
- `assets/video/close-1080.mp4` and `close-720.mp4`: the closing page's loop (Option 2 beach marina, 8.85 s, tail dissolved into head) cut from `../Extras/Closing-Video.mp4`, loaded when the page nears the viewport, with `assets/close-poster.jpg` as poster; `assets/logos/` holds the A365 Designs, Dantata Pyramids and Mizan Qist logos as transparent PNGs, each with a `-light` version recoloured for the dark closing video.
- `assets/video/hero-1080.mp4` and `hero-720.mp4`: the hero loop (H.264, no audio, 6.9 s, tail dissolved into head) cut from `../Hero-Video.mp4`; the script picks 1080p from 900 px wide and skips the video for reduced-motion and data-saver viewers, leaving the still image.
- `assets/gym-wide.jpg` and `gym-wide-s.jpg`: a sharpened 1856 × 1160 band cut from the full-resolution gym render for the full-width clubhouse banner on landscape screens (phones keep the portrait page render).
- `assets/plan/` the master layout in the light palette and the three floor plans as dark ink-on-alpha PNGs; `plan/units/` holds one mask per home plus its pool, balcony and backyard tints, cut by `../tools/build_units.py` (run after `build_assets.py drawings` and `build_footprint.py`). The residences explorer is a numbered plan sheet (key plans, room list, the home zoomed with tints) and "Inside a block" is an exploded program schematic drawn at runtime from the home outlines in `plan-data.js`; both follow `../Inspiration2/`.
- `assets/map/` the Location page's data: `places.js` (Lagos State and Eti-Osa outlines from OpenStreetMap, the Parkview Estate point) and `aerial.jpg` (Esri World Imagery tiles stitched and greyed), fetched once by `../tools/map/fetch_location.py`; both need their credits, which the page carries.
- Theme (since 2026-10-02, at the client's request): a grey field (#cbcbca, after page 5 of the inspiration book) with off-white panels and bands (#f6f6f4; Options, Architecture, Parks, Experience and Gallery are `section.band`), dark type (#1f2227) and the timber accent (#7d5428 for text, #c7955f on buttons); the hero video, the quote and labels over photographs keep light type on dark scrims. Drawings are rendered in dark ink and the master plan in a light palette by `build_assets.py`.
- The services page uses a Material Symbols Outlined subset from Google Fonts (`icon_names=` in the stylesheet link, weight 200, ligature names in `.msym` spans); add a new icon by adding its name to that link and a `.svc` tile.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis load from CDNs; the page degrades to a plain scrolling page without them and honours reduced motion.

Tooling lives one level up in `../tools/`: `build_assets.py` (renders and drawings from the project folder), `build_footprint.py` (footprint polygon for the schematic, run after `build_assets.py drawings`), `qa.py` (Playwright screenshots, `BASE=<url>`), `build_artifact.py` (single-file preview: page renders re-encoded as WebP, long side PREVIEW_PX=1500 at PREVIEW_Q=74, plans quantised, srcset stripped, to stay under the artifact's 16 MB cap; the preview is therefore softer than the site itself).

Serve locally with `python3 -m http.server 8765` from this folder. `robots.txt` and the `noindex` meta keep the page out of search until launch.
